Name policy

"Verstaan" and the Verstaan logo, when one exists, identify this project and its official releases.

You may use the name to refer to the project, to say your software is built with or compatible with Verstaan, and in unmodified redistributions of official releases.

Please do not use the name, or a confusingly similar one, as the name of a modified version, a fork, a product, a company or a domain, without written permission from the project owner. Describe a modified version as "based on Verstaan" instead.

Neither the MPL-2.0 engine licence nor the CC BY-SA 4.0 data licence grants trademark rights. MPL-2.0 section 2.3 and CC BY-SA 4.0 section 2(b)(2) say so explicitly.
