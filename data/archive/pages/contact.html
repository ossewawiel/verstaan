Contact

Contributions, corrections, and additional content to the UNL Archive are warmly welcomed. For inquiries, suggestions or submissions, please send an email directly to admin@unlarchive.org. If you are submitting a copyright/takedown notice, please include full details — see the guidelines at the bottom of this page.

Response time: The UNL Archive is a volunteer-run private initiative. We will respond as capacity allows; do not rely on the Archive for urgent or time-sensitive support.
Privacy & data: Messages may be retained to process requests. We will not share your personal data except to comply with legal obligations or to process copyright/takedown requests.

Takedown / Copyright requests

If you believe content posted on the Archive infringes your copyright, provide a written notice including:

  • Your contact details;
  • Identification of the copyrighted work;
  • Location or link to the allegedly infringing material;
  • A statement of good-faith belief the use is unauthorized;
  • Your signature (electronic acceptable).

Send takedown notices to admin@unlarchive.org.

Other ways to help / Contribute

The UNL Archive is a private, volunteer-run repository. If you want to help maintain, improve metadata, contribute preserved materials, or assist with technical work, please contact admin@unlarchive.org describing your skills and how you'd like to contribute.
