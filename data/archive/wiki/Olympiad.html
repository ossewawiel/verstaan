The UNL Olympiad is a series of competitions organised by the UNDL Foundation in order to foster the development of UNL-driven resources (dictionaries, grammars and corpora). As of July 2013, there has been two editions of the UNL Olympiad:

  • I UNL Olympiad, December 2012 - February 2013
  • II UNL Olympiad, March 2013 - July 2013
  • III UNL Olympiad, December 2013 - April 2014
  • IV UNL Olympiad, September 2014 - October 2014
