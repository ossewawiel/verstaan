The project WIKIPEDIA aims at creating dictionary entries corresponding to the titles of the Wikipedia.

Repository

[edit | edit source]

The project WIKIPEDIA is organized into six different repositories, according to the number of languages with translations for the English titles appearing in the Wikipedia.
