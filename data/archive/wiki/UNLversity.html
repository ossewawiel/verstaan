The UNLversity congregates the actions of the UNDL Foundation Training and Research Program. It consists of 4 initiatives dedicated to including new languages into the UNL framework and improving the quality of the current resources and specifications:

  • The UNL School, a series of workshops dedicated to the construction of UNL-driven dictionaries and grammars for less-resourced languages
  • The UNL Olympiad, a series of competitions to foster the development of UNL-driven resources
  • The UNL Research, a series of research and development projects to improve the quality of the current resources and specifications
  • The UNDL Foundation Internship in Computational Linguistics
