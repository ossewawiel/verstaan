The project Universal Library (UL) aims at translating, to the Universal Networking Language (UNL), the integral text of books thought to constitute an essential foundation in literature and philosophy. Our main goal is 1) to set standards and guidelines for human UNLization; and 2) to test several tools that have been developed at the UNDL Foundation. The resulting UNL document is also planned to be used in the evaluation of UNL-based translations, and as a training material for VALERIE, the Virtual Learning Environment for UNL.
